## Description (até 4000)

Nephelia is a fast first-person arena shooter set in a floating city of the early 1900s, high
above a sea of clouds.

Grab your revolver and jump into five-minute free-for-all matches across three city blocks
linked by bridges and aerial rails. Hook onto a zipline to cross the sky, fall on your rivals
from above and don't get knocked off the island: the drop is a long one.

FEATURES
• Frantic free-for-all matches: first to 15 eliminations or best score when the clock runs out
• Aerial rails: hook on, slide over the rooftops and let go wherever you want
• Revolver, lever-action repeater and shotgun, plus health tonics scattered around the city
• Smart bots with three difficulty levels, so there is always a fight, even offline
• Local multiplayer: play with friends on the same Wi-Fi. Your game shows up by itself on their
  screen, no typing and no account. Bots fill the empty spots
• A match that goes from a golden afternoon to a starry night: sunset, moon, and street lamps
  lighting up as the clock runs down
• Touch controls made for the phone, with adjustable sensitivity and button size
• Also plays with a game controller

No ads. No accounts. No data collected.

## App Review Information (notas para o revisor)

Nephelia works fully offline: tap PLAY to fight bots (no account, no login, no network needed).

MULTIPLAYER works between two devices on the same Wi-Fi network: on the first device tap
MULTIPLAYER > HOST GAME; on the second device tap MULTIPLAYER and then the game that appears
under "GAMES ON THIS WI-FI". The match starts by itself 3 seconds later, and bots fill the empty
spots. The first time, iOS asks for permission to find devices on the local network; this is only
used to find and join games on the same Wi-Fi.

Controls: left side of the screen moves, right side looks around; buttons for fire, jump,
reload and the aerial rail hook (it appears near a rail). Credits and licenses: OPTIONS > CREDITS.
