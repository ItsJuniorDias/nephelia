Nephelia – Support

A fast first-person arena shooter in a floating city of the early 1900s.

Need help, found a bug or have an idea? Email CONTACT_EMAIL. Please tell us your device model and iOS version. We usually answer within a few days.

How do I play?

Tap PLAY to fight bots. The left side of the screen moves, the right side looks around. Use the buttons to fire, jump and reload. Near an aerial rail a HOOK button appears: tap it to ride the rail and tap again (or jump) to let go. Health tonics and better weapons are scattered around the city. The first to 15 eliminations wins, or whoever has the most when the clock runs out.

How does multiplayer work?

Multiplayer works between devices on the same Wi-Fi network, with no account. On one device tap MULTIPLAYER and then HOST GAME. On the other devices tap MULTIPLAYER: the game shows up under "GAMES ON THIS WI-FI"; tap it to join. The match starts by itself a few seconds after someone joins, and bots fill the empty spots.

My friend's game doesn't show up

  • Make sure both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network (not mobile data).
  • Allow local network access: Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network > turn on Nephelia (on both devices).
  • Some public or guest Wi-Fi networks block devices from seeing each other. Try a home network or a phone hotspot.
  • Close and reopen the game on both devices, then try again.

Can I change the controls?

In OPTIONS you can change look sensitivity, button size, sound and music volume. The game also works with a game controller.

Does the game collect my data?

No. There are no accounts, ads or tracking. See the privacy policy.
