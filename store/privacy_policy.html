Nephelia – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Nephelia is a game made by Alexandre de Paula Dias Junior ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it. In short: we do not collect any personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no connection to our servers. We do not receive, store, sell or share any information about you.

Information kept on your device

Your options (look sensitivity, button size, volume, bot difficulty) and the player name you type are saved only on your device, so the game remembers them next time. Deleting the app deletes them.

Local multiplayer (same Wi-Fi)

When you use MULTIPLAYER, the game talks directly to other devices on the same Wi-Fi network to find and join games. Only what the match needs is exchanged between those devices: your player name and your moves in the game. Nothing goes over the internet to us or to anyone else. iOS asks for permission to find devices on your local network the first time; the game uses it only for this.

Children

Because the game collects no personal data, it does not collect data from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example, if online features are added in the future), we will update this page and the date above before the change reaches the game.
